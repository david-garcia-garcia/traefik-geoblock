IPinfo Lite (Country, ASN) is the free data download. Licensed under CC-BY-SA 4.0.

Comparison table lists city, region, region_code on the bundle schema; those columns belong to Core/Plus, not described as Lite-only fields on the Lite product page.

Field descriptions on this page swap the prose for `as_name` (“Organization domain name of the ASN”) and `as_domain` (“Name of the ASN organization”) relative to the Lite database page. Lite product page is the product-specific owner for Lite column meaning.
