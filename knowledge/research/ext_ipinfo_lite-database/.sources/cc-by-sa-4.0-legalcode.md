Section 2(a)(1): worldwide, royalty-free, non-sublicensable, non-exclusive, irrevocable license to reproduce and Share the Licensed Material in whole or in part, and to produce, reproduce, and Share Adapted Material.

Share includes distribution and making material available to the public.

Section 3: Attribution when Sharing; ShareAlike for Adapted Material (same license elements or BY-SA-compatible).

Section 4 (sui generis database rights): right to extract, reuse, reproduce, and Share all or a substantial portion of database contents; putting a substantial portion into another database makes that database Adapted Material.
