If you want to include data from MaxMind’s GeoLite databases in a product or service you provide to your users or customers, you need a Commercial Redistribution License for GeoLite.

That license allows distributing an unlimited number of copies of the GeoLite databases to customers and users as part of a single reseller product. Each additional product that incorporates the GeoLite database needs another license.

Restrictions include: hold customers/users to a EULA limited to “Internal Restricted Business Purposes”; do not allow or encourage using the data to identify a specific household, individual, or street address. Read the full Commercial GeoLite License on maxmind.com. Contact sales@maxmind.com for use-case guidance.
