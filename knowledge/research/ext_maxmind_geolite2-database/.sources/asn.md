Look up ASN and autonomous system organization for IPv4/IPv6. CSV zip: `GeoLite2-ASN-CSV_{YYYYMMDD}.zip`. Blocks columns: `network`, `autonomous_system_number` (integer), `autonomous_system_organization` (string). No country columns.

Dummy test MMDB: `GeoLite2-ASN-Test.mmdb` in maxmind/MaxMind-DB `test-data/`. May–August 2026 unpacked GeoLite ASN MMDB: 12.0–12.4 MB.
