Anyone who uses GeoLite databases or web services is covered by the GeoLite EULA unless they have a GeoLite Commercial License or a custom offline contract.

EULA incorporates CC-BY-SA 4.0 attribution if you share the material.

If you share GeoLite Databases or GeoLite Data, you must hold others to the same or substantially similar requirements, including the obligation to delete old GeoLite Databases.

You must maintain up-to-date data and destroy old versions. You must not use or encourage others to use GeoLite to locate specific people, households, or street addresses.
