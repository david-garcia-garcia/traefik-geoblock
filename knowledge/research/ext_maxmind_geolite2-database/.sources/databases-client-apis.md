Official client APIs: .NET (C#), C (libmaxminddb), Java, Node.js, PHP, Python, Ruby. No official Go library.

Unofficial APIs: “Use at your own risk.” MaxMind does not offer support. Go entries:

- oschwald/geoip2-golang — https://github.com/oschwald/geoip2-golang
- IncSW/geoip2 — https://github.com/IncSW/geoip2

`mmdbinspect` (beta, MaxMind, written in Go) is a CLI to look up IPs in MMDB files and print JSON. Not listed as a client library.
