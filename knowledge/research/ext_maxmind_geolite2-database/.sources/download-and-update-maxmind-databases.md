HTTPS required. R2 presigned URLs; allow `mm-prod-geoip-databases.a2649acb697e2c09b632799562c076f2.r2.cloudflarestorage.com`.

Every account is limited to 1,000 total direct downloads (30 for GeoLite accounts) in a 24-hour period. Requests that only check for updates do not count. Prefer downloading once to a local repository and distributing internally.

Direct download API hosts: `https://download.maxmind.com` and `https://updates.maxmind.com`.

Update schedule: GeoLite Country and GeoLite City — Tuesday and Friday. GeoLite ASN — every weekday (Monday–Friday). GeoIP Country/City — weekdays.

Account portal: Download Files under GeoIP/GeoLite. Archived versions available for 30 days after release.
