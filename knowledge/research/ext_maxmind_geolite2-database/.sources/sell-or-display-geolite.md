fetched: 2026-08-27
authority: vendor

EULA allows GeoLite data for “Internal Restricted Business Purposes” inside the business/org.

EULA also allows using GeoLite data to build applications available to users outside the company, or to display that data to users outside the organization, as long as you attribute the data to MaxMind.

Attribution example for advertising and documentation: `This product includes GeoLite Data created by MaxMind, available from https://www.maxmind.com.`

Do not use GeoLite to identify a specific household, individual, or street address.

Selling GeoLite data or applications that include them, if you do not want to include attribution, requires a GeoLite Commercial License.
