Product: look up second-level domain names associated with IPv4 and IPv6 addresses.

Binary field list is on the binary reference page. Dummy MMDB: `GeoIP2-Domain-Test.mmdb`. CSV zip `GeoIP2-Domain-CSV_{YYYYMMDD}.zip`. CSV columns: `network`, `domain`.

May–August 2026 unpacked MMDB size about 11.7–13.0 MB. New fields may be added at any time.
