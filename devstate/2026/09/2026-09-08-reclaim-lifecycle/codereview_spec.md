[wrong] `std_go_reclaim_context-lease` "Lifecycle events are logged" — change `specs/std_go_reclaim_context-lease/spec.md:117` ("Orphan and dispose SHALL use the logger from the last `Open` that bound that key") — `pkg/reclaim/table.go:182` — `Open` assigns `s.logger = logger` for every mapped slot before it reads the state, so an `Open` that only parks (`slotBusy`, line 194) or discards the slot (`slotGone`, line 205) also overwrites the logger of an incarnation it never binds to. `expire` re-reads `s.logger` at dispose time (line 346), so at zero grace the sequence "holder A bound with loggerA, drop sleeps the slot, Open B parks on `ready` and overwrites the logger, drop unmaps the key and calls `expire`" emits that incarnation's `reclaim_dispose` through loggerB, and B goes on to create a fresh incarnation instead. The same holds for a slot `Reset` unmapped while it was busy. Master could not do this: every `Open` that reached the assignment also bound. It also weakens the "Open logger level gates put and dispose" scenario, because a racing info-level `Open` can suppress another incarnation's dispose line.
   → Assign `s.logger` only where the Open actually binds: the `slotAwake` branch (line 185-188), `put` (line 231-237), and `reclaim` (line 249-257). Leave the `slotBusy` and `slotGone` branches read-only.

   Argument: Real. s.logger is now taken only where the Open binds: the awake branch and reclaimLocked, plus put for a fresh incarnation (ad8402b). TestTable_AnOpenThatOnlyWaitsDoesNotTakeTheLogger fails on the unconditional assignment.
